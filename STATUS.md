## M6 — Image-space road adjacency and scene visualization

The fixed contact policy samples central pavement immediately **below** each box, using 60% of box width and a band of max(2 pixels, 15% of box height). Pixel membership is integer-center, half-open and clipped. A region needs six pixels and at least half of its ideal area; predicted drivable overlap ≥0.50 yields `drivable_adjacency_detected`. Other valid regions yield `drivable_adjacency_not_detected`. Boxes touching image edges now yield `unknown_image_boundary` rather than a definitive result or an assertion of truncation. Explicit annotation hints distinguish `unknown_truncated` and `unknown_occluded`; insufficient coverage, missing road and invalid regions remain separately unknown. These are image-space evidence statuses, not occupancy probabilities or safety labels. Lane pixels are kept separate.

The typed scene joins unchanged M4 depth estimates, road evidence, model/checkpoint provenance and explicit calibration/visualization statuses. The camera overlay and strict JSON are generated on original pixels. A metric camera X–Z plot is produced only with verified rectified intrinsics and valid M4 selected-ray surface points. The bus image has no such calibration, so no 3D coordinates or metric X–Z output are produced. The separate synthetic fixture verifies X–Z points (−5,10) and (+10,20) m and equal metric axes.

The independent review reproduced a scene-consistency defect: associations computed from a positive road mask were accepted beside a same-identity all-zero replacement mask. Scene assembly now verifies deterministic source fingerprints covering detector identities/boxes, policy, both full-resolution road/lane mask contents and road provenance, then recomputes contact diagnostics using preserved visibility hints. Rendering validates the same pairing before drawing. The fingerprints enforce local data consistency, not model authenticity or accuracy. `matplotlib` is now a direct project dependency, compatible with the existing pinned Windows lockfile; the environment was not reinstalled.

A subsequent adversarial review found that an original M4 fusion result could be paired with detections whose instance mask pixels had changed or whose mask had disappeared, even when road associations were recomputed. Scene assembly checks it against the supplied detections; overlay rendering inherits that check. The road-association fingerprint remains box-only because its computation does not use instance masks. Legacy M4 results without this fingerprint remain valid standalone results but cannot be assembled into a new scene until fusion is rerun on the supplied detections. These hashes verify local pairing, not model authenticity or depth accuracy.

## M5-L1-R1 — Provenance and validated-input immutability correction

Validated scan arrays were copied and marked read-only, but NumPy could re-enable writes on their owned storage.

The protocol now retains immutable validated JSON bytes and checks their SHA-256 at construction. Its `data` property returns a fresh policy tree, so edits to scalar or nested values cannot affect evaluation. The approved v1 JSON remains byte-identical with SHA-256 `02451176def64af5ea83adc3c63df0ee37351176b008ec947f7da20c3f1f75fc`; the primary three-bin rule, 1/3/5/10-bin sensitivity set, tolerance and rejection policies are unchanged. Projection and sparse-reference rejection diagnostics are copied into immutable mappings; `SourceDigests` already held frozen strings.
